pgcli collects feedback on GitHub and by email.

- **Report a bug or request a feature** — open an issue at
  [mars-base/pgcli/issues](https://github.com/mars-base/pgcli/issues). Include
  the output of `pg version` and the steps to reproduce.
- **By email** — write to
  [dw_wang126@126.com](mailto:dw_wang126@126.com) if you prefer not to post
  publicly.
